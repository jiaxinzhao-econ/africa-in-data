Rate definition

Rates are local-currency units per US dollar. A higher value means local-currency depreciation; a lower value means appreciation.

Each country uses one daily series published by its national monetary authority. The source-specific definitions are listed below. Official series are not necessarily executable closing prices, and their calculation methods differ.

Data treatment

  • All available positive observations are included through the stated cutoff.
  • Missing dates remain missing; no forward filling or interpolation is used.
  • Parallel, cash, offshore and forward rates are not combined with the official series.
  • Ambiguous duplicate dates and non-positive source rows are excluded and disclosed in the manifest.
  • Observation date, retrieval timestamp, source identifier and quote convention are retained in each download.
