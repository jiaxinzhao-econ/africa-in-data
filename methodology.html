Methodology & sources

How to read and reuse this dataset.

The public beta favours a narrow, reproducible dataset over broad but ambiguous coverage. Every value carries a currency, date, market segment, definition and official source.

1. Core definition

Rates are expressed as local-currency units per US dollar. Therefore, a higher number means that more local currency is required to buy one dollar—a local-currency depreciation. A lower number indicates appreciation.

For each completed calendar month, the dataset selects the last available official observation from the configured national authority. The period end is the calendar month-end; the observation date records the actual publication or trading day used.

2. Comparability and limits

“Official” does not mean methodologically identical. South Africa is a bank-transaction- weighted reference, Egypt a market weighted average, Nigeria an NFEM volume-weighted average, Ghana an interbank mid, and Ethiopia an indicative weighted average.

  • The common elements are quote orientation, monthly selection rule and source authority.
  • Values are not assumed to be executable market closes.
  • Parallel, cash, offshore and forward rates are not mixed into these series.
  • Missing observations remain missing; no forward fill is used.
  • Source transitions are marked and should be considered when interpreting changes.
